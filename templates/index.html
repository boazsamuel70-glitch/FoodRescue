How FoodResQ Works

A simple process that connects surplus food with people in need.

1

Donate Food

Restaurants, hotels, caterers and households submit details of surplus food.

➜
2

Admin Reviews

The administrator verifies the request and assigns the nearest NGO.

➜
3

NGO Collects

The assigned NGO collects the food from the donor.

➜
4

Food Delivered

The food reaches people who need it before it goes to waste.

Why Choose FoodResQ?

We're making food donation easier, faster and more transparent for everyone.

Verified Requests

Every food request is reviewed and approved by the administrator before it becomes visible.

Trusted NGOs

Food donations are assigned only to registered NGOs to ensure safe collection and distribution.

Live Tracking

Donors can track every stage of their donation from submission to successful delivery.

Reduce Food Waste

Prevent perfectly edible food from ending up in landfills by donating it to people who need it.

Secure Process

Every donation and request passes through an organized approval process managed by the administrator.

Community Impact

Every donation contributes to feeding hungry people and building a stronger community.
