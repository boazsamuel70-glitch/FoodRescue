Donation Guidelines

  • ✔ Donate fresh and hygienically prepared food.
  • ✔ Enter the actual quantity available.
  • ✔ Select the exact pickup location.
  • ✔ Ensure someone is available during pickup.
  • ✔ Pack the food properly.
  • ✔ Mention allergens or special ingredients.
  • ✔ Provide accurate best-before information.
