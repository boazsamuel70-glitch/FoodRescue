About FoodResQ

Turning Surplus into Sustenance through a transparent, reliable and community-driven food donation platform.

Our Story

Every day, large quantities of perfectly edible food are wasted while many individuals and families struggle to access a proper meal.

FoodResQ was created with a simple goal — to bridge the gap between surplus food and people in need. By connecting donors, NGOs and administrators on one platform, FoodResQ ensures that food reaches the right people through an organized, transparent and accountable process.

Together, we believe every meal saved is another life touched.

Our Mission

To minimize food waste by creating an efficient platform where individuals, restaurants, hotels, caterers and NGOs can donate surplus food quickly and responsibly.

Our Vision

To build a future where no edible food goes to waste and every person has access to nutritious meals through community participation and technology.

How FoodResQ Works

1

Donation Submitted

Donors submit details of surplus food through the platform.

2

Admin Verification

Every donation is reviewed before being assigned.

3

NGO Assignment

The administrator assigns the nearest suitable NGO.

4

Food Collection

The assigned NGO collects the donated food.

5

Distribution

Food is distributed to people or organizations in need.

6

Double Confirmation

The donor confirms the food was handed over.

The receiver confirms the food was received.

Only then does the administrator mark the donation as completed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can donate food?

Individuals, restaurants, hotels, caterers and NGOs can donate surplus food.

Can NGOs donate food?

Yes. NGOs can both donate surplus food and receive food from other donors.

Who verifies donations?

Every donation is verified by the administrator before being assigned to an NGO.

How is delivery confirmed?

Both the donor and the receiver must confirm the successful delivery before the administrator marks it as completed.

Is FoodResQ free?

Yes. FoodResQ is completely free for donors, NGOs and food requesters.
